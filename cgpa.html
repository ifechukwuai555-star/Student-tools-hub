How CGPA is calculated

CGPA is calculated by multiplying each course's grade point by its credit units, adding all the quality points together, and dividing by the total credit units.

CGPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credit Units

Frequently Asked Questions

How is CGPA different from GPA?

GPA normally represents performance for a particular semester or period, while CGPA represents your cumulative performance across multiple semesters.

Can I add multiple courses?

Yes. Use the Add Course button to add as many courses as you need.

What grading scale does this calculator use?

The default scale is A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1 and F = 0.
