How the CGPA calculation works

Each course contributes points based on its grade and credit units.

CGPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credit Units

For example, a 3-unit course with an A contributes 3 × 5 = 15 quality points.

Frequently Asked Questions

What grading scale does this calculator use?

It uses a 5-point scale by default: A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1 and F = 0.

Can I add multiple courses?

Yes. Use the Add Course button to add as many courses as you need.

Does credit unit affect CGPA?

Yes. Courses with more credit units have a larger effect on the calculated result.
