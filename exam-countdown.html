How It Works

Choose your exam date and time. The calculator compares that time with your device's current local time and updates the countdown every second.

If the selected exam time has already passed, the calculator will tell you that the exam time has passed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the countdown update automatically?

Yes. The countdown updates every second while the page is open.

Which time zone does it use?

It uses the local date and time configured on your device.

Can I use it for any event?

Yes. Although it is designed for exams, you can use it for any future date and time.
