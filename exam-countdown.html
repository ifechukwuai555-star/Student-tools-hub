How it works

Choose your exam date and time, then select Start Countdown. The timer uses your device's local time and updates every second.

If the exam time has already passed, the countdown will show that the exam has passed instead of displaying negative values.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the countdown update automatically?

Yes. Once started, the countdown updates every second while the page is open.

Which time zone does it use?

It uses the local date and time configured on your device.

What happens when the exam time arrives?

The timer stops at zero and displays a message that the exam time has arrived.
