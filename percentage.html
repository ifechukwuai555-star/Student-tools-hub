How Percentage Calculations Work

To find what percentage one value is of another, divide the value by the total and multiply by 100.

Percentage = (Value ÷ Total) × 100

To find a percentage of a number, multiply the percentage by the number and divide by 100.

Percentage of a Value = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Value
