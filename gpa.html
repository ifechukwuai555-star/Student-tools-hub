How GPA is calculated

GPA is calculated by multiplying each course's grade point by its credit units, adding the quality points and dividing by the total credit units.

GPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credit Units

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GPA?

GPA is a grade-point average calculated from the courses included in a particular semester or academic period.

Is GPA the same as CGPA?

No. GPA normally represents a particular academic period, while CGPA represents cumulative performance over multiple academic periods.

Can I add more courses?

Yes. Tap the Add Course button to add additional courses.
