How the GPA calculation works

GPA is calculated by multiplying each course's grade point by its credit units, adding the quality points, and dividing by the total credit units.

GPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credit Units

GPA normally represents a specific semester or study period, while CGPA represents the cumulative result across multiple periods.

Frequently Asked Questions

What grading scale does this calculator use?

It uses the 5-point scale: A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1 and F = 0.

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA usually represents one semester or study period. CGPA represents your cumulative performance across multiple periods.

Can I add more courses?

Yes. Tap Add Course to add additional courses before calculating your GPA.
