How It Works

The calculator correctly accounts for different month lengths and leap years when calculating dates.

New Date = Starting Date + Number of Days

For example, entering 30 adds 30 days. Entering -30 subtracts 30 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it account for leap years?

Yes. The calculator uses the calendar date system, including leap years.

Can I subtract days?

Yes. Enter a negative number, such as -7, to subtract seven days.

Can I calculate the days between two dates?

Yes. Use the Difference Between Dates section at the top of this page.
