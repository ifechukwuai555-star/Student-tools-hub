How it works

The Date Calculator uses calendar dates to calculate the exact number of days between two dates.

You can also enter a starting date and a whole number of days. Positive numbers move forward, while negative numbers move backward.

The calculator correctly handles different month lengths and leap years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the calculator handle leap years?

Yes. The calculator uses real calendar dates, including leap years.

Can I subtract days?

Yes. Enter a negative number of days to move backward from the starting date.

Can I calculate the number of days between dates?

Yes. Enter the start and end dates in the first section and select Calculate Difference.
