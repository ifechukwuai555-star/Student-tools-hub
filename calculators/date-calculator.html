How It Works

Select two dates to calculate the number of days between them. You can also choose a starting date and add or subtract a number of days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it account for leap years?

Yes. The calculator uses JavaScript date calculations, which account for leap years and different month lengths.

Can I subtract days from a date?

Yes. Select “Subtract days” and enter the number of days.

Can the date difference be negative?

The calculator shows the absolute difference between the two selected dates.
