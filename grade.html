Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest possible grade?

A score from 70 to 100 receives an A.

Can I enter a decimal score?

Yes. Decimal scores such as 74.5 are supported.

What happens if I enter an invalid score?

The calculator will ask you to enter a valid score between 0 and 100.
