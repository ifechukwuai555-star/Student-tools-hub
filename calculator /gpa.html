How GPA Is Calculated

GPA is calculated by dividing the total quality points by the total credit units for the selected courses.

Quality points are calculated by multiplying each course's credit units by its grade point.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the GPA formula?

GPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credit Units.

What grading scale does this calculator use?

It uses the common 5-point scale: A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1, and F = 0.

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA usually represents a particular semester or academic period, while CGPA represents cumulative performance across multiple periods.
